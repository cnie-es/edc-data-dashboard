LANGUAGE DATA SPACE STANDAR LICENSE

PREAMBLE

In order to be permitted to enter into this Language Data Space Standard Licence (the "Licence"), Participants need to be able to access the Language Data Space (LDS) marketplace by adhering to the LDS General Terms and Conditions. Participants to the LDS act as data providers or as data recipients at each data transaction.

The LDS structure is fully decentralised. According to the LDS General Terms and Conditions, Participants have acknowledged and agreed that, as of now, LDS does not implement quality checking measures of data exchanged on LDS. However, Data Providers must guarantee that data that they offer through the Language Data Space is under their full ownership or include clear and appropriate distribution rights. Data providers do not upload their data to central repositories. The data is transferred directly from the providers' infrastructure to that of the data recipient. The data provider can freely decide when, under which conditions and with whom they will share the data. Upon execution of this licence, each party receives a copy of the Licence via its respective connector. The existence, metadata and transaction details of the licence are logged through the LDS central monitoring component.

Each party acknowledges that the licence has been developed by LDS with the objective of achieving an appropriate balance between the respective rights and obligations of the LDS participants, in order to facilitate the use of the LDS marketplace, promote more efficient exchanges between participating data providers and data recipients, and foster the development of a data market. The parties acknowledge that the LDS marketplace operates across the EU. French law is chosen as the governing law of the Licence, taking into account the organization and governance of LDS. The provisions of the Licence have therefore not been unilaterally predetermined by either participant, and therefore Article 1171 of the French Civil Code shall not apply.

In light of the foregoing, the Participants hereby agree as follows.


DEFINITIONS

Licence: This Language Data Space Standard Licence



Connector: The software provided by LDS that enables Participants to formulate offers, access, and exchange Data and data services with other Participants. The connector facilitates automated data transmission and interoperability between systems in accordance with the LDS General Terms & Conditions

Data: Any digital representation of acts, facts or information and any compilation of such acts, facts or information, including in the form of text, sound, visual or audiovisual recording, which may be Personal Data and/or Non-Personal Data

Data Provider: Participant, including public sector bodies and international organizations, which, in accordance with applicable Union or national law, has the right to grant access to or to share Data exclusively by using the Connector

Data Recipient: Participant, acting for purposes which are related to that person's trade, business, craft or profession, to whom the Data Provider makes Data available exclusively by using the Connector

LDS General Terms and Conditions: agreement defining the roles, relationships, rights and obligations of LDS participants.

Non-personal data: Data other than Personal Data

Participant: either of the Parties committed to the governance framework of LDS and having a set of rights and obligations stemming from this framework and which has adhered to the LDS General Terms & Conditions

Party: Any legal person that has entered into this Licence as either Data Provider or Data Recipient Personal Data: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ('data subject'); an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that natural person

Representatives: as applicable in relation to a Party, its directors, officers, employees, contractors, in each case who have access to or use any Data in the course of the performance of the Licence. Reference to Data Provider or Data Recipient necessarily includes the representatives of each.

Third-Party Rights: Third parties' rights on Data resulting from, among others, legislation on Personal Data protection, intellectual property rights (copyright and related rights, the sui generis database right), trade secrets, rights resulting fron the Data Act, rights resulting from Data Governance Act, rights from contractual obligations of confidenciality.
